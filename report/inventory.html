    System Classification

    Two complementary ways to group crawled pages. URL templates (from path shape) show how the site organises its URLs — parametric segments like [id], [slug], [date], [year], [uuid], [hash] collapse automatically when ≥3 values share a shape. DOM similarity (from 64-bit tag-path simhash) groups pages by structural near-duplication; raise the tolerance slider to catch pages that differ by a few modules.
